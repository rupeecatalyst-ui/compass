# Policies

Enterprise policies (credit, eligibility, risk models, property configuration) are administered from **Credit & Risk Engine** and related Administration modules — not from Marketing or informal docs.

## How to open policy administration

1. Administration → Administration Console.
2. Search **Credit & Risk** or open the AI / Credit category modules.
3. Use Policy Library, Rule Library, Eligibility Models, Risk Models, and Product/Lender policy desks as provided.

## Chanakya vs Policy Engine

- **Chanakya** advises, mentors, and surfaces readiness — **never blocks** workflow.
- Only the **Enterprise Policy Engine** may hard-block business actions.
- Chanakya may explain a policy; it must never become the policy.

## Decision Ledger

Important configuration and commercial decisions should leave an **Enterprise Decision Ledger** record (append-only). Do not silently overwrite historical rule versions that past transactions depended on.

## Warnings

> Do not disable Continue / Back CTAs based on Chanakya readiness scores. That is a constitutional violation.
