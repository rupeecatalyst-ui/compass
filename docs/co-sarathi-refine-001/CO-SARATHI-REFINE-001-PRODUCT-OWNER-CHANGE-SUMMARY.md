# CO-SARATHI-REFINE-001 — Product Owner Change Summary

## What changed for customers

1. SARATHI opens as a financial intelligence partner — not an “Enterprise AI Platform” demo.
2. Conversation starts by understanding the need; recommendations wait.
3. Questions adapt to the product the customer mentions — one at a time.
4. Before advice, SARATHI shows a short summary and asks for confirmation.
5. Suggested next steps appear only after that confirmation.
6. Engineering words (Draft, Platform, CRM Disabled) stay out of the primary desk.

## What did **not** change

- Enterprise AI Platform engines, Policy Gate, Domain Boundary, Action Proposal execution model
- Auth / certification admin credentials
- Primary navigation architecture (still `/sarathi`)

## Review path

1. Open `/sarathi`
2. Use a starter (e.g. Home Loan)
3. Answer the clarifying question(s)
4. Confirm the summary
5. Review suggested next steps

## Certification stance

Ready for Product Owner UX review on Vercel. Not a new engine certification sprint.
