# CO-SARATHI-UX-001 — Conversation Flow Examples

Mandatory flow: Understand → Think → Ask ONE question → Reply → … → Confidence → Summary → Confirm → Recommend → Action Proposals.

## Loan Against Property

Customer: I need a Loan Against Property.  
SARATHI: I'd be happy to help. May I know what you intend to use the funds for?

Customer: Business expansion.  
SARATHI: Understood. Is the property residential or commercial?

Customer: Residential.  
SARATHI: Thank you. Approximately how much funding are you looking for?

…continue until confidence ready → **Here's what I understand.** → confirm → recommendations.
