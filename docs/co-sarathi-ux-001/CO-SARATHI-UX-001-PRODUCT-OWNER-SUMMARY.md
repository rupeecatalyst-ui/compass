# CO-SARATHI-UX-001 — Product Owner Summary

## Intent

Make SARATHI feel like an experienced financial consultant — not a questionnaire or CRM wizard.

## Delivered

- Natural welcome  
- One Planner question per turn (in the message)  
- No mid-chat fixed question chips  
- Slogan / repetition filtering  
- Consultation Confidence gate before summary  
- Action Proposals only after confirmation  
- Messaging-style conversation UI  

## Not changed

Enterprise AI Platform · Policy Gate · Context Intelligence · Planner architecture · Financial Decision Engine · Tool Bus

## Review

Open `/sarathi` on the production Vercel URL and walk Home Loan, LAP, Business Loan, Balance Transfer, Personal Loan, and an outside-domain question.
