# CO-WP-103B — Wealth Partner App UX Constitution

**Status:** PRODUCT OWNER DIRECTION · CRITICAL · Governing  
**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Not a feature sprint** — product direction for all remaining WP App development.

## Philosophy

The Wealth Partner App is a **Daily Business Companion**, not a CRM, MIS, reporting app, dashboard, or mobile Catalyst One.

| Catalyst One | Wealth Partner App |
|--------------|--------------------|
| Runs the business | Helps the partner succeed |
| Decides content & entitlements | Renders the experience |
| Enterprise Experience Engine | Presentation only |

Partners should want to open the app multiple times every day. The app inspires generation — it does not display reports.

## Design

Premium · Minimal · Elegant · Modern FinTech · Interactive  

No dense tables · no enterprise dashboard clutter · large cards · rounded corners · generous space · gradients · meaningful motion.

## Home (experience, not MIS)

Greeting · Hero Carousel · My Business Today · Business Feed · Recommended Actions · Today's Highlights · Digital Visiting Card · Compact Saarthi  

**Never on Home:** pipeline / deals / opportunities / commissions as KPI widgets.

Business numbers live on **Business**.

## Morning test

> Would a Wealth Partner enjoy opening this every morning?

No → redesign.

## Enterprise Experience Engine

Home visuals are Experience Engine projections (hero, feed, campaigns, training, creatives, announcements, notifications, actions, highlights, toolkit). Companion must not become the content SSOT.
